**Spec: Why the quadratic formula?**

1. Tiles: ax² + bx + c laid out as tiles; divide by a, and each algebra line appears beside the tile move that makes it.
2. Missing corner: the x² square plus two (b/2a)x strips leaves a gap of exactly (b/2a)², which you add to both sides.
3. Square root: the completed square gives (x + b/2a)² = (b² − 4ac)/4a², and the ± appears because both sides of the square count.
4. Check: sliders for a, b and c, with roots marked on a live parabola so the formula's answer visibly lands on the axis crossings.
