**Spec: Why do the three equations of motion come from one picture?**

1. Graph: a straight v–t line from v₀ with slope a.

**Spec: Why can horizontal and vertical motion be treated separately?**

1. Twin drop: one ball dropped, one thrown sideways at the same moment, with strobe images every 0.1 s.

4. Combine: x = v cos θ t and y = v sin θ t − ½gt² give the parabola.

**Spec: Galileo's double incline: why would the ball roll forever?**

1. Setup: a ball released on the left slope rises to the same height on the right (no friction).
2. Flatten: lowering the right slope makes the ball travel further to reach that height.
3. Limit: at 0° the height is never reached, so the motion never stops: inertia.
4. Reality: a friction slider shows why real balls stop.

**Spec: Why do only some forces have a potential energy?**

1. Loop: move a block around a closed path on a hill, with and without friction.
