C turns stretches into forces. Aᵀ adds up the forces on each mass. So the whole system is K u = f with K = AᵀCA. K is symmetric and, with a wall at both ends, positive definite. Free the right end and K can lose that, if the whole row of masses can slide.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
