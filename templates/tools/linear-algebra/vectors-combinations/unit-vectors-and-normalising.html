{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Divide a vector by its length and see its tip land on the unit circle.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Dividing v by |v| keeps the direction of v and changes the length to 1. Its tip lands exactly on the unit circle, so it is a unit vector. This is how any direction can be described with a vector of length 1. The zero vector has no direction, so it cannot be normalised.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
