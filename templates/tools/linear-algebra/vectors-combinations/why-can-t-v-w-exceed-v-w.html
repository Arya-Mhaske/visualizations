{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Turn w and watch its shadow on v stay inside the circle of w.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The shadow of w on the line of v is one side of a right triangle whose longest side is w itself. A shorter side can never be longer than the longest side. So |v·w| = |v| × shadow ≤ |v||w|. The two are equal only when w lies along v, where the shadow is all of w.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Turn w a quarter-turn from v. How long is the shadow?
  • Does the ratio |v·w| ÷ |v||w| ever go above 1?
