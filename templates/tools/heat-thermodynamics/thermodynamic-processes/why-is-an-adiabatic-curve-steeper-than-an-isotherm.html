{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Two ways to expand the same gas from the same start: keep its temperature fixed, or let it cool.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}On an isotherm the temperature is held, so only the volume change lowers the pressure: P ∝ 1/V. On an adiabat no heat enters, so the gas that does work cools as it expands, and its pressure falls for a second reason. That is why PV^γ = const falls faster, steeper by the factor γ at the crossing point.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
