{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Count the degrees of freedom of a molecule and read off Cᵥ, Cₚ and γ.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Each degree of freedom adds ½R to Cᵥ, and Cₚ = Cᵥ + R. So γ = 1 + 2/f: 5/3 for a single atom (f = 3), 7/5 for a rigid diatomic molecule (f = 5), and 4/3 for a non-linear polyatomic (f = 6). Air is mostly diatomic, so γ ≈ 1.4.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
