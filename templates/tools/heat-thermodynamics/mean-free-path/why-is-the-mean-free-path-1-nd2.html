Count them in the tube it sweeps.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}In a distance L the molecule sweeps a tube of volume πd²L. With n molecules per unit volume the tube holds n πd²L of them, so it collides that many times. One collision per path λ gives λ = 1/(nπd²). Doubling the diameter makes the tube four times wider, so λ falls to a quarter. Doubling n halves it.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
