Squeezed hard, molecules have size: they cannot be packed into zero volume, so the pressure is higher (V − b). Cold and close, they attract each other: they hit the wall less hard, so the pressure is lower (a/V²). The ideal law is good only when the gas is thin and hot.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
