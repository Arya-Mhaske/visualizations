{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Measure the pressure of a fixed-volume gas at several temperatures and extend each line backwards to zero pressure.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Whatever the gas and however much of it, the straight lines meet the temperature axis at the same place, −273.15 °C. A gas cannot have less than zero pressure, so that is the lowest temperature: absolute zero.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}

Real gases turn liquid before they reach this point, so the lines are extrapolated, not measured all the way. The scatter in the points is made up by a seeded random generator.
