{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Sunlight passes through the atmosphere, but the ground's infrared does not pass back out so easily. Raise the layer's absorption and watch the ground warm.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}In balance, as much energy must escape to space as the Sun delivers. A layer that absorbs infrared sends part of it back down, so the ground must radiate more to get the same energy out. More radiation means a higher temperature, because radiated power goes as T⁴. With a = 0 the ground is at −18 °C. With a ≈ 0.78 it is about 15 °C.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
