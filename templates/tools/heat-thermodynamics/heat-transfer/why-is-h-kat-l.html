Change the lanes, the length and the temperature difference.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Each lane carries the same flow. Twice the lanes (area A) means twice the flow. Twice the temperature drop means twice the push, so twice the flow. Twice the length spreads the same drop over a longer path, so the slope is halved and so is the flow. Together: H = KAΔT/L, where K says how easily the material carries heat.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
