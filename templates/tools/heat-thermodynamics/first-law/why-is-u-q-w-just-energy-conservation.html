{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}A gas in a cylinder is an energy account. Heat pays in, work paid out, and the balance is the stored energy.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Energy cannot be created or destroyed. Heat Q brought into the gas adds to its stored energy U. Work W done by the gas on the piston takes energy out. What is left is the change: ΔU = Q − W. The first law is energy conservation, written for a gas.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Add 400 J of heat and let the gas do 0 J of work. Where does the energy go?
  • Insulate the gas (Q = 0) and let it do 200 J of work. What happens to its temperature?
  • Make W negative (the piston is pushed in). What does that do to U?
