See what happens near 4 °C.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Cooling water makes it denser, so cold water sinks and the whole lake cools together, until it reaches 4 °C. Below 4 °C water gets less dense, so the cooler water stays on top, and ice forms there first. The 4 °C water at the bottom stays liquid, so fish survive.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}

Each step removes 1 °C of heat from the top layer only, and denser water sinks. A simplified model, since heat also leaks by conduction.
