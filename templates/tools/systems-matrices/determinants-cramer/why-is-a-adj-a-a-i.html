Row 2 times the cofactors of row 1 is the same expansion but of a matrix whose first row has been replaced by row 2. That matrix has two equal rows, so its determinant is 0. So A (adj A) has |A| on the diagonal and 0 everywhere else.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
