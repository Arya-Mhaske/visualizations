So a product can change sign only where one of its factors is zero (or, for a rational expression, where the denominator is zero). Between two critical points the sign never changes, so one test point tells you the sign of the whole region. A squared factor such as (x − 2)² never changes sign at all.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
