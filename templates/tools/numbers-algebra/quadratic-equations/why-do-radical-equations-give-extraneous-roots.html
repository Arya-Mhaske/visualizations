Some answers belong only to the mirror.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The square root symbol means the positive root only. Squaring both sides loses that: x + p = (x + q)² also holds when the left side was −√(x + p). Graphically, y = √(x + p) and y = −√(x + p) join into one sideways parabola. The line can meet the lower half, and those answers are extraneous. Always check every answer in the original equation.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Try p = 1 and q = 1. Why are both answers genuine this time?
