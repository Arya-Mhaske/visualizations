It crosses the axis twice, once or not at all.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The vertex of y = ax² + bx + c sits at height −(b² − 4ac)/4a. For a > 0 the parabola opens upward, so it crosses the axis exactly when the vertex is on or below it, which is when b² − 4ac is positive or zero. At zero the vertex just touches the axis, and the two roots become one. The discriminant is the vertex height in disguise.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Set a = 1, b = 0 and slide c from −5 to 5. When does the vertex touch the axis?
  • Choose any b, then find the c that gives exactly one crossing.
  • Check: at that c, what is b² − 4ac?
