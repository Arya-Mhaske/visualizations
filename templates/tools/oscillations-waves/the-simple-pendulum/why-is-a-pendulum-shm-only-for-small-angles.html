A small swing keeps pace with the ghost. A big one falls behind.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The pull-back along the arc is mg sin θ, not mg θ. SHM needs a force proportional to θ, and sin θ follows θ only while θ is small. Higher up, sin θ is smaller than θ, so the pull is weaker than SHM would give and the swing takes longer.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
