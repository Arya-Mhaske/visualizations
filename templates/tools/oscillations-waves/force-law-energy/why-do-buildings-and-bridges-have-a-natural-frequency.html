Shake its ground at different frequencies and see when it sways most.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A building has a natural frequency f₀ = (1/2π)√(k/m), just like a mass on a spring. Shaking near f₀ pushes it in step with its own swing, so each push adds to the last. That is resonance. A taller building has more mass, so a lower f₀. Damping lowers the peak.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}
Simplified modelOne stiff frame with the floors lumped together, shaken steadily. Real buildings have several natural frequencies, and real earthquakes are not a single steady frequency.
