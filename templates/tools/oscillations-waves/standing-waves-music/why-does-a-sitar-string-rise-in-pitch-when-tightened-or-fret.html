Watch the pitch change, and hear it.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The lowest note is the wave that fits one half-wavelength between bridge and fret: λ = 2L, so ν = v/2L. Tightening raises the wave speed, v = √(T/μ), so ν goes up. Pressing a fret shortens L, so ν goes up as 1/L. Halve the length and the pitch doubles: one octave.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Press Octave. What happened to L, and what happened to ν?
  • With the open string, make the tension four times bigger (20 N to 80 N). How many times higher is ν?
  • Find two different settings of T and L that give the same pitch.
