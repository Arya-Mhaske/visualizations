An open end lets the air swing freely, so it must be an antinode. From a node to the next antinode is a quarter of a wavelength, so the pipe must hold 1, 3, 5 … quarter-waves: odd harmonics only. An even number would end on a node at the open end.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
