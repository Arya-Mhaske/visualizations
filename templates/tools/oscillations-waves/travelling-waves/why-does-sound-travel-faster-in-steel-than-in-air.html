Then compare how stiff and how dense each one is.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Speed is √(stiffness ÷ density). Steel is about a million times stiffer than air, but only about 6000 times denser. The stiffness wins, so the square root of the ratio is about 15: sound is 15 times faster in steel.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}
Which stiffness?In a gas it is γP. In a liquid it is the bulk modulus B. In a thin solid rod it is Young’s modulus Y. The animation runs 20 times slower than real life. Values: steel Y = 2.0 × 10¹¹ Pa, ρ = 7800 kg/m³; aluminium Y = 7.0 × 10¹⁰ Pa, ρ = 2700 kg/m³; water B = 2.2 × 10⁹ Pa, ρ = 1000 kg/m³.
