Change the current and the turns, and see how much stronger the iron makes it.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The field inside a coil grows with the turns per metre and the current: B = μ₀nI. Soft iron adds its own magnetisation, so the same coil gives a field hundreds of times larger. The lifting power grows as B², until the iron is saturated and more current helps little.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Raise the current to 3 A with 400 turns. Does the iron field still double when you double the current?
  • Compare the two bars. How many times more mass does the iron core lift?
