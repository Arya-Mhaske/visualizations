Raise the voltage on the cable and see how much of that is wasted as heat on the way.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The power sent is P = VI, so raising V lowers the current needed. Halve I and the heat in the cable, I²R, falls to a quarter. That is why transmission lines run at tens or hundreds of kilovolts, and transformers step the voltage down again near homes.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
