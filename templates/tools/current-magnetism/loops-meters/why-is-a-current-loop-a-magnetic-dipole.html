Zoom out and see them become the same.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Every piece of the loop adds its own bit of field, and far away those bits add up to the pattern of a small bar magnet. The strength of that magnet, its dipole moment, is m = NIA: more turns, more current or a bigger area all make a stronger magnet. Seen from far enough, a loop is a dipole.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
