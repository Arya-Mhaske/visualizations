{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}A concave mirror magnifies your face when you stand close. A convex mirror shrinks the road, and shows you far more of it.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Inside its focus, a concave mirror gives an erect, enlarged virtual image, which suits shaving. A convex mirror's curve turns the edge rays outward, so it takes in a wider angle than a flat mirror of the same size. The price is that everything looks smaller.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Move your face back from the shaving mirror. Where does the image turn upside down?
  • Bring the car closer. At what distance does the flat mirror lose it?
  • Change R. Does a more curved mirror show more road or less? What happens to the car's size?
