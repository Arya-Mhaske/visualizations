{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Choose two lenses, place them on the bench, and check the magnification.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Telescope: the objective makes an image at its focus, and the eyepiece should sit one fₑ beyond it. Then the final image is at infinity and m = f₀/fₑ. Move the eyepiece and the image comes closer and the magnification changes. Microscope: the objective makes a real, enlarged image, and the eyepiece magnifies it, so M = m₀ × (25/fₑ).{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Move the eyepiece 1 cm closer to the objective. What happens to the final image?
  • Microscope: how far from the objective does the slide go for the lenses you chose?
