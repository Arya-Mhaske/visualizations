Measure its two paths, subtract, and say bright or dark before you read the answer.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}At any point the two waves start together but travel different distances. If the paths differ by a whole number of wavelengths, crests meet crests and the point is bright. If they differ by a half number, a crest meets a trough and the point is dark. The shading shows that rule at every point.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
