Raise the angle and watch the part that escapes fade out.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Going from glass to air the ray bends away from the normal. The more slanted the incoming ray, the closer the outgoing ray gets to skimming along the boundary. At the critical angle C it just grazes it: sin C = 1/n. Past C there is no refracted ray that satisfies Snell's law (it would need sin r > 1), so all the light is reflected. It is trapped.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
