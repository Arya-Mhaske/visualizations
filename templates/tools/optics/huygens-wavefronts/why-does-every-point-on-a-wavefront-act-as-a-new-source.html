Draw its wavelet a moment later and see what line they make together.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Each point of a wavefront disturbs its neighbours, so each sends out a wavelet. Far from their sources the wavelets cancel, and only where they all touch a common line do they add up. That line is the next wavefront, a distance vt ahead. More sources make it smoother.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
