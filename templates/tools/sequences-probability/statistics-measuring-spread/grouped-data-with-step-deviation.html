{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}For grouped data, take an assumed mean A and class width h and work with u = (x − A)/h. Smaller numbers, same answer.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Shifting by A and dividing by h changes the mean to A + h·ū and the variance to h² times the variance of u. The result is the same whatever A you choose, but with a good A the numbers u are small whole numbers, so the arithmetic is much easier.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Compare the sizes of the u values.
