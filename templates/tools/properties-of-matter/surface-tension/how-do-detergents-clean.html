{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Greasy fabric has tiny pores that plain water will not enter. Add detergent and see what the water needs to get in.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Water meets a greasy pore at an obtuse angle, so its surface pushes back with a pressure 2S|cos θ|/r. Detergent lowers S and also the contact angle. Once cos θ turns positive, the water is drawn in by itself and lifts the dirt.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}

The way S and θ fall with the detergent amount is an illustrative model: S falls from 72 to 35 mN/m and θ from 110° to 60°, levelling off at the critical concentration (CMC).
