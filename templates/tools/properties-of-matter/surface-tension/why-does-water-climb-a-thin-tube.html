{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Dip a thin tube in a liquid. The meniscus pulls the liquid up until the column's weight balances the pull.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The surface pulls up on the glass round the whole circle: force = S cos θ × 2πr. The column that rises weighs πr²hρg. Balance them and h = 2S cos θ/(ρ g r). A thinner tube has less liquid to lift per unit of rim, so the liquid climbs higher. For mercury θ is obtuse, so cos θ is negative and the level drops.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Make the tube thinner. What happens to h?
  • Switch to mercury. Which way does the level go?
  • Compare the upward pull and the weight of the column.
