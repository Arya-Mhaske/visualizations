The same pressure acts on both, but a bigger area gives a bigger force.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Pascal's law: pressure applied to an enclosed fluid is passed on undiminished. With the same pressure P = F₁/A₁ = F₂/A₂ on both pistons, F₂ = F₁(A₂/A₁). The price is distance: the small piston moves A₂/A₁ times as far, so the work in equals the work out.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
