{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Pull a wire and it gets longer, and also a little thinner. The ratio of the two strains is Poisson's ratio.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The atoms are pulled apart along the wire, but the bonds sideways are not stretched; the material draws in to keep roughly the same volume. The lateral strain is −σ times the longitudinal strain, with σ about 0.3 for metals and close to 0.5 for rubber, which keeps its volume almost exactly.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}
