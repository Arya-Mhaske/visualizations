Follow the curve from Hooke's law region to fracture, and see what happens on release.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}In the proportional region stress ∝ strain (Hooke's law) and the wire returns completely. Past the elastic limit it comes back with a permanent set; at the yield point it stretches a lot for little extra stress; at fracture it breaks. Engineers keep loads in the linear region, well below the yield point.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}

The curves are idealised sketches. The strain axis for steel and copper is not to scale, because the straight Hooke region is really only a fraction of a per cent wide. Real curves depend on the sample and its treatment.
