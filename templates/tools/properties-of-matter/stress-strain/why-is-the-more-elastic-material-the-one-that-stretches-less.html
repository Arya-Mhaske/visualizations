{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Hang the same load on a steel wire and a rubber cord of the same size. Which one returns to its shape better, and which stretches more?{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Elasticity is the power to resist deformation and return. It is measured by the stress needed per unit strain, Young's modulus Y = stress/strain. Steel needs about 4000 times more stress than rubber for the same strain, so the steel stretches far less. By this measure steel is the more elastic material.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Raise the load. Which stretches more?
  • Compare Y for the two materials.
  • Make the wire thinner. What happens to both stretches?
