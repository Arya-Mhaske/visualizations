The thin one is closer to breaking.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}What the material feels is not the total force but how much force each bit of cross-section carries. Stress = F/A. A thin wire has less area so each square millimetre carries more force, and when the stress passes the breaking stress the wire snaps. Strain is the same for equal stress, whatever the thickness.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
