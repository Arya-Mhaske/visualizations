{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}A ball moving through air while spinning curves sideways. The spin drags a layer of air round with it.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}On one side the surface of the ball moves with the air stream, on the other side against it. The air is faster on one side and slower on the other, so the pressure is lower on the faster side and the ball is pushed towards it. This is the Magnus effect, or dynamic lift. Reversing the spin reverses the swing.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}

The size of the sideways force here uses a simple model proportional to spin and speed, chosen to show the effect clearly, not measured for a particular ball.
