{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Water leaves a hole at the bottom of a tank. Watch the level fall fast, then slowly.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Torricelli's law says water leaves at speed √(2gh), so the level falls at a rate proportional to √h. Separating gives dh/√h = −c dt, so √h falls in a straight line. The level itself falls quickly when the tank is deep and slowly when it is shallow.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}
Note This model treats the hole as ideal and the tank as a straight cylinder. Real holes lose some flow to friction and a narrowing jet, so real tanks take a little longer to empty.
