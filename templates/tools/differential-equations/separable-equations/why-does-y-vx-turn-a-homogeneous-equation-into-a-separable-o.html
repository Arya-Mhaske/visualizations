{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}In a homogeneous equation the slope marks are the same all along any ray from the origin.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A homogeneous equation has dy/dx = F(y/x). So every point on the ray y = vx has the same slope, F(v). Zooming in or out does not change the picture at all. Because the slope depends on one number v, write y = vx: then v alone decides the slope, and x and v can be separated.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Move v. Do the three slopes at the points on the ray stay equal to each other?
  • Change the window half-width. Does the pattern of marks change?
  • Pick y′ = (x + y)/x. What is the slope along the ray v = 1?
