{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}You half-remember m = m₀/√(1 − v²), and c has gone missing. Put it back so the formula makes sense.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The "1" is a pure number, and you can only subtract things of the same kind, so what is taken from 1 must be a pure number too. v² has units of m²/s². To cancel them, divide by something with those units: c², the square of a speed. That also fixes the fact that mass should not depend on the direction of travel, so v/c alone will not do, even though it is dimensionless.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Choose v/c and v²/c². Both pass the unit test. Which one passes the "+v and −v" test?
  • With v²/c², bring v/c close to 1. What happens to the mass?
