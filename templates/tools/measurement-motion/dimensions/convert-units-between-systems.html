{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}A constant's number depends on the units. Rewrite a calorie, G or g in new units for mass, length and time.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A quantity with dimension [M^a L^b T^c] changes by the ratio of each unit raised to its power: n₂ = n₁ (M₁/M₂)^a (L₁/L₂)^b (T₁/T₂)^c. A smaller unit of length makes a bigger number, and it does so b times over. So the same energy is 4.186 J in SI and about 4.186 × 10⁷ erg in CGS, where mass is in g, length in cm and time in s.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
