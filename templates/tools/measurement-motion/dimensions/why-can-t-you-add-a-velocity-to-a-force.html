{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Treat units like algebra. Add two quantities, or multiply them, and see which combinations make sense.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A sum only makes sense between things of the same kind: 3 m + 5 m = 8 m, but 3 m + 5 s says nothing. A velocity and a force have different dimensions, so there is no unit for their sum. Multiplying has no such rule, because it just builds a new quantity: N × m is a new thing, energy. So every real equation must have the same dimension on both sides of the equals sign and of each plus sign.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Add speed and force. Why is that meaningless?
  • Add energy and work. Why does that work?
  • Multiply force by length. What quantity has that dimension?
    • {% endblock %} {% block notes %}
    Note M, L and T stand for mass, length and time. Work and energy are measured in joules and have the same dimension, which is why they can be added.
