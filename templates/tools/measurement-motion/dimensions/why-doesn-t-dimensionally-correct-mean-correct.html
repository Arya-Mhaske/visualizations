{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Two formulas for kinetic energy pass the dimension test. Only one is true. Check them against a falling ball.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A dimension check can only see units, and a pure number such as ½ or 3/16 has no units. So k m v² passes for any k at all. Dimensions can reject a wrong formula, but they cannot approve a right one: they leave pure numbers, and any function of pure numbers, completely free. To find k you must measure, or derive it. Here a falling ball gains m g h and v² = 2gh, so only k = ½ matches.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
