The two forces always cancel, but they do not act along the same line.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The forces are +qE and −qE, equal and opposite, so the dipole's centre stays put. They act along two lines a distance 2a sin θ apart, which makes a couple. Its torque is qE × 2a sin θ, or pE sin θ, and it turns p towards E.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Set θ to 0° and 180°. Both have no torque. Press Play after a small nudge from each. Which is stable?
  • Double the length and halve the charge. What happens to p and to the torque?
  • Does the net force change with any control?
