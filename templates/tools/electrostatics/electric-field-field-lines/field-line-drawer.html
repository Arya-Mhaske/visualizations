{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Place charges, then drag them about. The lines are traced point by point, following the field.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Each line follows the direction of the total field. Lines start on positive charges and end on negative ones, and the number at a charge is proportional to its size. If the total is not zero, the extra lines go off to infinity.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Load +2 and −1. How many lines miss the −1 charge?
  • Load Two alike. Where do lines avoid each other?
  • Make the total charge zero. How many lines go off to infinity?
  • Drag a charge right next to another. How does the far picture change?
    • {% endblock %} {% block notes %}
    Note This is a flat slice of a 3D field. The directions are exact, but the spacing of lines on a slice only roughly shows the strength. A few lines may leave the drawing and come back.
