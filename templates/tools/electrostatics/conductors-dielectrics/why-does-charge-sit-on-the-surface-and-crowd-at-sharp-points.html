Where does the charge go?{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Joined by a wire, the two spheres are one conductor at one potential: kq₁/R₁ = kq₂/R₂, so the bigger sphere holds more charge. But charge per area, σ = q/4πR², is larger on the small sphere, by R₁/R₂. The field at a surface is σ/ε₀, so it is strongest at sharp points. That is why lightning rods are pointed and why sharp points leak charge into the air.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
