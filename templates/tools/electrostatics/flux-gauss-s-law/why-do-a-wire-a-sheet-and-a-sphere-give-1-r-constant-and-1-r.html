The area then decides the fall-off. A sphere has area 4πr², so E ∝ 1/r². A cylinder has side area 2πrL, so E ∝ 1/r. A pillbox through a sheet has the same flat end area at any distance, so E does not change.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
