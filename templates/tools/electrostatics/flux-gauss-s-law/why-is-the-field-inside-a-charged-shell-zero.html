{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block stage_class %}viz-stage--tall{% endblock %} {% block lead %}Draw a Gaussian sphere inside and outside a charged shell and see what Gauss's law says.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}By symmetry the field is the same size everywhere on a sphere around the centre and points straight out. So Gauss's law gives E · 4πr² = q_enc/ε₀. A sphere inside the shell contains no charge, so E = 0 there. Outside, q_enc = Q, so E = kQ/r², as if all the charge sat at the centre.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
