The area it meets grows as r², but the field falls as 1/r², so the flux through the piece is the same wherever the surface is. A dented surface only changes where each cone is cut, not how much flux it carries. A charge outside sends in as many lines as it takes out, so the net is zero.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
