See how thinly they spread as you go away.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The lines from a charge spread over a sphere of area 4πr². The same lines over four times the area at twice the distance means a quarter of the line density, and the field and force go the same way: F ∝ 1/r². Gravity has the same shape, because its field lines also spread over spheres.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
