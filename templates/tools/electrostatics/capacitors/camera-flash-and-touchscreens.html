A touchscreen senses a finger by the capacitance it adds.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A capacitor stores ½CV² and can give it all back very fast, because its discharge time RC is tiny: about a millisecond for a flash. The same energy delivered over a millisecond is thousands of watts. A touchscreen grid of tiny capacitors changes slightly where a conducting fingertip nears them, and the controller finds the biggest change.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
