{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Put more charge on a capacitor and see what its voltage does.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Doubling Q doubles the field between the plates (E = σ/ε₀ and σ = Q/A), and doubling E doubles V = Ed. So Q/V never changes as Q changes: it is fixed by the plate area and the gap. We call it the capacitance, C = Q/V, in farads. A bigger area or a smaller gap gives a larger C: a flatter line, which means more charge for each volt.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
