{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Keep the charge fixed, then widen the plates and narrow the gap.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Charge Q spread over area A gives σ = Q/A and a field E = σ/ε₀ between the plates. The voltage is that field times the gap, V = Ed. So C = Q/V = ε₀A/d. More area thins the charge and weakens E. A smaller gap shortens the distance the field acts over. Either way less voltage holds the same charge.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Double A. What happens to E, V and C?
  • Halve d. Which of E and V changes?
  • Check that C = ε₀A/d matches Q/V.
