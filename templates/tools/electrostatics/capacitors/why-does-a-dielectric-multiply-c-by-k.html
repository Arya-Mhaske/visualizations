The same charge now sits at lower voltage, and C = Q/V is K times larger. Half in, half out is two capacitors side by side, one with C₀ and one with KC₀.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Try K = 10. Is the change bigger?
  • Stop half way. Check C = C₀(0.5 + 0.5K).
