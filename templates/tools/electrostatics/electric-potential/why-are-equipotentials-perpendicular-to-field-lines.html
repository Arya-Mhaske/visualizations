Which directions cost no work?{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Work = force × distance moved along the force. Move along an equipotential and the potential does not change, so no work is done, so there is no field component along that direction. The field must therefore be at 90° to the equipotential. Field lines and equipotentials always cross at right angles.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
