{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Carry a test charge round a loop and add up the work on each side.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Along the sides that go outwards and inwards the force does work, and the two cancel because the force depends only on r. The sides that are at a steady distance cost nothing. Total work round any loop is zero. So the work from A to B is the same on every path, and we can define a potential. A swirling force fails this test.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Which sides do the work, and which contribute nothing, for the Coulomb force?
  • Switch to the swirling force. What is the total now?
