{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Potential is the work to bring a unit charge in from infinity: the area under the field curve.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Work = ∫E dr. The field of a point charge, kQ/x², integrates to kQ/r, so the potential is 1/r even though the field is 1/r². The area out to infinity is finite because 1/x² falls fast. For a line charge the field is 1/x and the area has no limit, so there V cannot be set to zero at infinity.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
