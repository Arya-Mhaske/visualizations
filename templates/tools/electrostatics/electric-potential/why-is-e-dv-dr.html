{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Potential is a landscape. A positive charge rolls downhill, and the steepness is the field.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The work done by the field over a small step dx is E dx, and it equals the drop in potential energy, −dV. So E = −dV/dx. A steep slope means a strong field. The minus sign says the field points downhill, towards lower potential. Where the landscape is flat, the field is zero.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Choose "+ and +". Find the point between the charges where V is lowest. What is E there?
