{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Slide off a car seat, step out, and touch the door. Will it shock you?{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Rubbing against the seat separates charge, and your body holds it. A tiny charge on a tiny capacitance makes a large voltage, V = Q/C. Air breaks down at about 3 kV per millimetre, so a few kV make a visible spark when you near the metal. Humid air lets the charge leak away within seconds, so winter and dry days are worst.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Slide 3 times at 30% humidity and wait 5 s. What is V?
  • Raise the humidity to 70%. What happens to V?
  • Touch the door at once, with a metal key in your hand. Does the shock change in size or only feel different?
