{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Charge an electroscope by touch, and use it to find the sign of an unknown charge.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Both leaves get the same sign of charge, so they repel and spread. A rod brought near only separates the charge in the metal, and the leaves fall back when it leaves. Touching shares real charge, which stays. A + test rod then makes the leaves spread more on a + electroscope and collapse on a − one: that is how the sign is found.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
