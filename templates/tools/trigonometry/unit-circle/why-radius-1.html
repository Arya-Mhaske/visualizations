{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Make the circle bigger or smaller and keep the angle. What changes, and what does not?{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A bigger circle stretches the whole picture. Both x and y grow by the same factor as r, so x ÷ r and y ÷ r do not change. The ratios belong to the angle and not to the circle. If we choose r = 1, the division does nothing, and the coordinates themselves are cos θ and sin θ. That is the only reason for the unit circle: it removes a division.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
