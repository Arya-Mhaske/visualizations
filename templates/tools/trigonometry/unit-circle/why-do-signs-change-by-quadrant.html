Watch the signs of x and y, and of sin, cos and tan.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Cosine is the x-coordinate and sine is the y-coordinate, so their signs are just the signs of x and y. Right of the y-axis x is positive, above the x-axis y is positive. Tangent is y ÷ x, so it is positive when x and y agree in sign. The rule "All, Sin, Tan, Cos" is only a picture of that, so there is nothing to memorise.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Go to the third quadrant. Why is tan positive when both x and y are negative?
  • Cross the y-axis. Which sign changes, and which does not?
