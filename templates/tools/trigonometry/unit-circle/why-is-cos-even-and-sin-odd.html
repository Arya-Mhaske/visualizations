{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Turn the angle one way, then the same amount the other way. Compare the two points.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Turning by −θ sends the point to its mirror image across the x-axis. A mirror in the x-axis keeps x the same and flips the sign of y. Cosine is x, so cos(−θ) = cos θ, which is the mark of an even function. Sine is y, so sin(−θ) = −sin θ, which is the mark of an odd function.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Set θ = 55°. Read cos θ and cos(−θ). Then sin θ and sin(−θ).
  • Use a negative angle for θ. Does the pattern still hold?
  • Use tan θ = sin θ ÷ cos θ. Is tan(−θ) equal to tan θ or to −tan θ?
