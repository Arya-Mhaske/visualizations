See what happens to the tension as the cables flatten.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The two cables share the weight through their upward parts. Each pulls up with T sin φ, so 2T sin φ = W and T = W / (2 sin φ). As φ gets small, sin φ gets small, so T gets large. A perfectly flat cable would need infinite tension.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Set φ = 30°. How does T compare with W?
  • Lower φ to 5°. How big does T get?
  • Raise the weight. Does the shape of the graph change?
    • {% endblock %} {% block notes %}
    Note The cables are treated as straight, light and perfectly stiff. Real cables stretch and sag.
