The ground track is the vector sum.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The plane moves through the air and the air moves over the ground. Add the two velocity vectors tip to tail and the sum is the velocity over the ground. A crosswind does not just slow the plane; it also pushes it off its heading.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Set the wind to 0. What are the ground speed and the track?
  • Fly north with a wind from the west. Which way is the track pushed?
  • Fly into a head wind. What happens to the ground speed?
