{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Multiply the conjugate of z by w and look at the real part.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Multiplying (a − bi)(c + di) gives (ac + bd) + (ad − bc)i. The real part ac + bd is exactly the dot product of the arrows ⟨a, b⟩ and ⟨c, d⟩. The imaginary part ad − bc is the cross term, which tells the signed area of the parallelogram. This is where the dot product came from historically.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Make z and w perpendicular. What is the real part?
  • Swap z and w. What happens to the imaginary part?
