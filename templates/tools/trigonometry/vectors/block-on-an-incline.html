{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Tilt a slope and split the weight into a part along the slope and a part into it.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Weight acts straight down, but the block can only slide along the slope. Split it into two parts at a right angle: mg sin θ along the slope, and mg cos θ into the slope. The angle θ of the slope reappears between the weight and the perpendicular, which is why sin and cos appear.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Set θ = 0°. How big is the part along the slope?
  • Raise θ to 45°. How do the two parts compare?
  • Set θ close to 90°. What is the part pressing into the slope?
