{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Lift the axle of the spinner. Watch the whole wave rise with it.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The tip's height above the axle is sin x. If the axle itself is k above the ground, the height above the ground is k + sin x. Every point of the wave moves up by the same k. The shape, the period and the amplitude stay the same. Only the middle line moves, from y = 0 to y = k.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Set k = −0.5. Where is the middle line now?
