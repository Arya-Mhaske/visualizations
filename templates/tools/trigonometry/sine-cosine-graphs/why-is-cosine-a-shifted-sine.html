{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Cosine is the sideways shadow of the same spinner. Put a second arrow a quarter turn ahead and compare the two waves.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The cosine of θ is the x-coordinate of the point, which is the height of an arrow that is a quarter turn (π/2) ahead. So the cosine wave is the sine wave moved a quarter of a cycle to the left, as if it started early. Both are the same shape, one cut from the other. cos θ = sin(θ + π/2).{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • At θ = 0, what is the height of each arrow? Which wave starts at its top?
  • Step forward to θ = π/2. What are the heights now? Which wave has reached the middle?
  • Find a moment when the two waves cross. What is θ?
