{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Watch an arrow spin on a circle. Its height, drawn against the angle, makes the sine graph.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The sine of an angle is the height of the point on a unit circle. As the arrow turns steadily, that height rises to the top, falls through the middle to the bottom and comes back. Drawing the height against the angle gives a wave. It repeats every full turn, so the wave has period 2π. The wave is just the circle, unrolled.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Stop at θ = π. Where is the arrow? What is the height?
  • Go to 2π. What has the curve done, and where is the arrow?
