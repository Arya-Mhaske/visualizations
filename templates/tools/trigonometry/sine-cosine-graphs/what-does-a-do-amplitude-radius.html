What happens to the wave?{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The height of the tip is the radius times the sine of the angle: y = A sin θ. When the circle grows, every height grows by the same factor, but the arrow still takes the same time to go round. So the wave becomes taller and stays just as wide. A is called the amplitude, and it is simply the radius of the circle.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
