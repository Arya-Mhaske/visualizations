The unit circle shows it for every t.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}In a right triangle with hypotenuse 1 and one leg t, one acute angle has sine t and the other has cosine t. The two acute angles add to 90°, or π/2. The circle shows more: the points for arcsin t and arccos t are mirror images in the 45° line, because swapping x and y swaps sine and cosine. So the sum is π/2 even when t is negative.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
