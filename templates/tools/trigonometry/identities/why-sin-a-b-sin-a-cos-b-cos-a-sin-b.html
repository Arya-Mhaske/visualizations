{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Stack one right triangle on another. The height of the top corner is two pieces.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The first triangle has hypotenuse cos B and angle A. The second sits on its end with hypotenuse 1 and legs cos B and sin B. Its far corner is at angle A + B on the unit circle, so its height is sin(A + B). A horizontal line through the join cuts that height into cos B sin A and sin B cos A. Adding them gives the formula, and no algebra was needed.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Set A = 30° and B = 60°. Check that the two pieces add to sin 90° = 1.
  • Make B small. Which piece nearly disappears?
  • Read the x-coordinate of the top corner. Which formula is that?
