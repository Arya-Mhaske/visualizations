{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Early astronomers measured arcs with chords, like a bowstring across a bow. Half of that chord is what we now call the sine.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Indian astronomers worked with half-chords, and the Sanskrit word for them meant bowstring. The word was copied into Arabic by sound, as jība, which meant nothing there. Arabic is written without short vowels, so later readers took it for jaib, a word that means "fold" or "pocket". Latin translators put in their own word for a fold, sinus. We shortened that to "sine".{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
