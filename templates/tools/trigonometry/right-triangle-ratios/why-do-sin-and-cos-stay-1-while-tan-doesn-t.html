Watch the three values.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Sine and cosine divide a leg by the hypotenuse. The hypotenuse is always the longest side, so these fractions can never pass 1. Tangent divides one leg by the other leg. As θ nears 90° the adjacent leg shrinks towards 0, and dividing by something tiny gives something huge. So tan has no upper limit.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
