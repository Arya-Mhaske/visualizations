See what happens to sine and cosine.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The two acute angles of a right triangle add up to 90°, so each is the complement of the other. From corner A the vertical leg is opposite. From corner B the same leg is adjacent. So sin of one angle is cos of the other: cos θ = sin(90° − θ). "Cosine" is short for "complement's sine".{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
