Match the precision of the angle with the figures you keep.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}If an angle is only known to within half of its last unit, the side you compute from it can be wrong by a matching share. A share of about 1% means two figures are worth keeping. A share of about 0.1% means three. This gives the rule in the book: angle to the nearest 1° pairs with 2 figures, 10′ with 3, 1′ with 4 and 10″ with 5.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
