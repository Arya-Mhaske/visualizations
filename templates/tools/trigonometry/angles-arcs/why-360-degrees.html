History, not proof Babylonian astronomers counted in base 60, so six parts × 60 = 360 is the most widely accepted historical explanation. Historians still debate it.
Plausible, not proven Some ancient calendars used 360 days plus extra festival days. The link to 360° is plausible but not proven.
Next, the unit that comes from the circle itself: Why radians are the natural unit →
{% endblock %} {% block why %}
So, why? Nothing in nature forces 360. It was chosen because it splits evenly so many ways (24 different group sizes, against 4 for 365), and it fits base-60 counting and the roughly 365-day year. Later, mathematicians found a unit that does come from the circle itself: the radian.
