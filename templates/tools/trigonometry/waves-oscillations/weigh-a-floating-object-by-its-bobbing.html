Time one bob and work back to the mass.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A floating block is pushed back by the water with a force that grows with how far it is pushed down. That gives a sine motion with period T = 2π√(m / ρgA). Turn it round and m = ρgAT² / 4π², so a stopwatch weighs the block.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Raise the mass. Does the period get longer or shorter?
  • Raise the area. What happens to the period?
  • Double the mass. By what factor does T change?
