Once they are in place, each piece between two of them has the same shape: it crosses the axis halfway and reaches ±A a quarter of the way along. A sets the steepness, B sets how far apart the asymptotes are (π ÷ B), and C slides everything sideways. There is no amplitude, only a stretch.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
