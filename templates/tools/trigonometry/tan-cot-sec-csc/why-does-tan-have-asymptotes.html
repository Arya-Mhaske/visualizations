Then see what that does to the graph.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}At 90° the ray points straight up, parallel to the tangent line, and the two never meet. Just before 90° they meet very high up, and the closer the ray gets to vertical, the higher the meeting point. The graph of tan θ therefore climbs without limit and never reaches the vertical line at 90°. That line is an asymptote.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Slide θ from 60° to 89°. How does the meeting point move? What does the dot on the graph do?
  • Slide θ to −89°. Which way does the meeting point run off now? What sign does tan θ have?
  • The graph has a second dashed asymptote. Which angle is it at, and why is it also an angle where the ray cannot meet the line?
