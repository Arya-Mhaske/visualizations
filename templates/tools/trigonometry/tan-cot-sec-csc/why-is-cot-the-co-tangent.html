Where does the same ray meet it?{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Measured from the vertical axis, the ray makes the complementary angle, 90° − θ. By the same picture as before, the length on the top tangent line is tan(90° − θ). We call it cot θ, the "co-tangent", just as the cosine is the complement's sine. The graph of cot is the tan graph seen in a mirror at 45°, and cot θ × tan θ = 1.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
