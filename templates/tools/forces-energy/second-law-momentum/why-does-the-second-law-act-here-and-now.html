What does it do sideways afterwards?{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The second law relates the force now to the acceleration now. After release there is no horizontal force on the stone, so its horizontal acceleration is zero and it keeps the velocity it had at the moment of release. The train goes on accelerating and leaves it behind.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
