{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Move a block from A to B by two different routes, and then round a closed loop. Compare gravity with friction.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Gravity's work depends only on the start and end heights, so round any closed loop it sums to zero. That is what lets us define a potential energy. Friction's work depends on the length of the path, so it is not zero round a loop, and no potential energy can be defined for it.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Look at the closed loop. Which work is zero?
