{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}A ball sits on an energy landscape. The force on it points downhill, and is larger where the slope is steeper.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}V(x) is the energy stored at x. If the ball moves a little, the energy changes by dV, and the work done by the force is F dx = −dV. So F = −dV/dx: the force is minus the slope of the landscape. A flat bottom (slope 0) means no force: an equilibrium.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Put the ball on a steep part. Which way is the force?
  • Find the points where the force is zero.
  • Compare the steepest slope with the force there.
