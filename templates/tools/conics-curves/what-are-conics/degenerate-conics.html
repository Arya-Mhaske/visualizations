The curve collapses to a point, a line, or two lines.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A plane that misses the apex cuts a proper conic. A plane through the apex cuts the cone only along lines through that point. If the plane is flatter than the cone's side it touches the cone only at the apex: a point. If it is exactly as steep as the side it lies along one side of the cone: one line. If it is steeper it cuts through the cone along two lines. These are the degenerate conics, the limits of the ellipse, parabola and hyperbola.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
