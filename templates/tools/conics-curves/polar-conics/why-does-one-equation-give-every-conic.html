{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}One equation, r = ℓ/(1 − e cos θ), and the eccentricity decides which conic you get.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Every conic is the set of points whose distance from the focus is e times their distance from the directrix. That one rule gives one equation in polar form. When e is 0 it is a circle. Below 1 it is an ellipse. At 1 it is a parabola, and above 1 it is a hyperbola. The same formula covers all four, which is why astronomers use it for orbits.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
