{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block stage_class %}viz-stage--flow{% endblock %} {% block lead %}Convert a conic between polar and rectangular form.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Multiply out the polar equation to get r = ℓ + e r cos θ. Replace r cos θ by x and square both sides, using r² = x² + y². That gives a quadratic equation in x and y, with the coefficient of x² equal to 1 − e². Its sign tells you the type at once: positive for an ellipse, zero for a parabola, negative for a hyperbola.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Use e = 0.5. What is the coefficient of x²?
  • Set e = 1.0. What happens to the x² term?
  • Switch the direction and read the eccentricity back out of the polar form.
