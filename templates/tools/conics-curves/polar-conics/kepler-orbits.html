{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}The Sun is at a focus of the orbit: the planet moves fast near it and slow far away.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Kepler's second law says a line from the Sun to the planet sweeps out equal areas in equal times. Near the Sun the line is short, so to sweep the same area the planet must move fast. Far away the line is long, so the planet moves slowly. The ratio of the fastest and slowest speeds is (1 + e)/(1 − e).{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Set e = 0. What is the speed everywhere?
