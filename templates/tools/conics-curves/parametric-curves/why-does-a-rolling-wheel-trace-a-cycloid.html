{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block stage_class %}viz-stage--wide{% endblock %} {% block lead %}Roll a wheel and trace a point on its rim.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}When a wheel of radius r rolls without slipping, its centre moves at the same speed as the wheel turns: after turning by θ, the centre has moved rθ. A point on the rim goes round the centre while the centre goes forward, so its position is (rθ − r sin θ, r − r cos θ). This curve is the cycloid: arches with cusps where the point touches the ground.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
