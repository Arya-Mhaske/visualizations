{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Roll one circle round another and draw epicycloids and hypocycloids.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A point on a small circle rolling round a large one traces a curve with sharp points called cusps. Rolling outside gives an epicycloid. Rolling inside gives a hypocycloid. If the radii are in the ratio p : q in lowest terms, the curve has p cusps. When r = R/4 inside, you get the astroid with four cusps.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Roll inside with R = 4 and r = 1. How many cusps?
  • Roll outside with R = 3 and r = 1. What shape is it?
  • Try R = 5 and r = 2. How many times must it go round before it closes?
    • {% endblock %} {% block notes %}
    Note A cardioid is the epicycloid with R = r. Spirograph toys make these curves.
