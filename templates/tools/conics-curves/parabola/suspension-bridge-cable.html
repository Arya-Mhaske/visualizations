{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block stage_class %}viz-stage--wide{% endblock %} {% block lead %}Fit a parabola to the towers and the sag, then read the cable height at any point.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A cable that carries a bridge deck of equal weight per metre hangs in a parabola, y = x²/4a, measured from its lowest point. The towers are at x = ±L/2 and are the sag higher, so (L/2)² = 4a · sag, which gives a = L²/(16 · sag). The height of any hanger follows from the equation. A cable that hangs under its own weight alone is a slightly different curve, called a catenary.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
