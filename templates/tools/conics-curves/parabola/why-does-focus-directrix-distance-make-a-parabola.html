{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Keep a point equally far from a fixed point and a fixed line. It traces a parabola.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A parabola is every point that is the same distance from the focus F as from the directrix, a line. Drag P around and look at the two distances. Only on the curve are they equal. Inside the curve P is closer to F, and outside it is closer to the line. Placing P with the distances equal and leaving a trail builds the curve one point at a time.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Turn on "Keep the distances equal" and drag P up and down. What shape does the trail make?
  • Turn it off and put P inside the curve. Which distance is smaller?
  • Increase a. Does the parabola get wider or narrower?
