The receiver must sit exactly at its focus.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}For a dish of width D and depth d, the parabola is x² = 4fy with (D/2)² = 4fd, so the focus is at f = D²/(16d) above the vertex. A deeper dish has a closer focus. The receiver must be at that height, or the signals will miss it. A headlight is the same shape used backwards: a lamp at the focus sends parallel light out.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Make the dish deeper. Where does the focus move?
  • Switch to the headlight. What happens to the rays when the lamp is off the focus?
