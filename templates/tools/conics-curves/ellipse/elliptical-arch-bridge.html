Will the boat fit?{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The top half of the ellipse x²/a² + y²/b² = 1 gives the arch height y = b√(1 − x²/a²). The arch is highest in the middle and falls away towards the banks. A boat fits only if the arch is high enough above both of its top corners, so a boat that clears the middle can still hit the arch near the edge.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Put the boat in the middle with a height of 4 m. Does it fit?
  • Slide the boat towards the bank. At what position does it stop fitting?
  • Make the boat narrower. Can it get closer to the bank?
