{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Change the eccentricity from Earth's orbit to a comet's and watch the ellipse stretch.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A planet's orbit is an ellipse with the Sun at one focus. The eccentricity e = c/a says how stretched it is. Earth's e is about 0.017, so the orbit is almost a circle. Halley's comet has e near 0.97 and swings in close to the Sun and far out again. The nearest and farthest distances are a(1 − e) and a(1 + e).{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
