Its diagonal swings down to land on the foci.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Draw a rectangle that reaches a to each side of the centre and b up and down. The diagonals are the asymptotes. The diagonal has length √(a² + b²). Swing it down to the axis with a compass and it lands on the foci, so c is the diagonal and c² = a² + b². For the ellipse c² = a² − b², and the sign change comes from the right triangle being built from the other side.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
