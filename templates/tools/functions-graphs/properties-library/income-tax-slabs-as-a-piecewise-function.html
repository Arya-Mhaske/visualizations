The graph is a chain of straight pieces, one for each slab.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Each slab taxes only the rupees inside it, at its own rate. So the tax rises along a straight piece, and the slope of that piece is the slab's rate. When income enters the next slab, the slope steps up. There is no jump, because the graph is joined, and your average rate is always lower than the rate on your last rupee.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
