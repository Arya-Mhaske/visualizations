{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block stage_class %}viz-stage--wide{% endblock %} {% block lead %}The vertex sits midway between the roots, or wherever completing the square puts it.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A parabola is symmetric about a vertical line through its vertex. The two roots are mirror images in that line, so the vertex is halfway between them. The roots are −b/2a plus and minus the same amount, so their average is −b/2a. Even without real roots, the same symmetry puts the vertex at x = −b/2a.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Choose a parabola with two roots. Is the vertex halfway between them?
  • Make the parabola miss the axis. Is the vertex still at −b/2a?
  • Change a. Does the vertex move?
