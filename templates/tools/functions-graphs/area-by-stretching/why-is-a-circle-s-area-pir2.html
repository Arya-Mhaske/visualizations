A ring at distance d from the centre unrolls into a strip of length 2πd. Stacking the strips from the smallest to the largest makes a triangle with base 2πr and height r, whose area is ½ × 2πr × r = πr². The thinner the rings, the closer the stack is to a perfect triangle.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
