{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Move a skater's arms in and out and watch the moment of inertia and spin rate change.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The moment of inertia I = ∫r² dm counts each bit of mass by the square of its distance from the axis. Pulling the arms in cuts that distance a lot, so I drops a lot. With no outside torque, L = Iω stays fixed, so ω must rise.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}
Note The skater is modelled as a uniform disk with two point masses on the arms. The numbers are round values, not measurements.
