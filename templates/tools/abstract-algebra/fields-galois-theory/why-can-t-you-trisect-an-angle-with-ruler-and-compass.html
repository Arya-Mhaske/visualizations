{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Check whether the cubic that cos(θ/3) must satisfy has a rational root, and what that means for ruler and compass.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Each ruler-and-compass step solves at most a quadratic, so constructible lengths have degree 1, 2, 4, 8 … over the rationals. To trisect 60° we need cos 20°, a root of 8y³ − 6y − 1 = 0. That cubic has no rational root, so it is irreducible of degree 3, and 3 is not a power of 2. The angle cannot be trisected by this means.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}
Note This tool shows the argument for angles whose cosine is rational. It does not prove that no ruler-and-compass trisection exists for every angle; it shows why 60° is the standard impossible case.
