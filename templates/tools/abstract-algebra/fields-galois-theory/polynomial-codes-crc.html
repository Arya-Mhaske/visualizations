Dividing by the generator leaves a remainder that is appended to the message. The sent word is then a multiple of the generator, so any single flipped bit leaves a non-zero remainder at the receiver and is caught.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}
Note Real CRCs use longer generators and are tuned for the kinds of burst errors that occur in practice.
