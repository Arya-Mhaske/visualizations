{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Follow the chain of commutator subgroups down from a Galois group and see whether it reaches the trivial group.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A polynomial can be solved with roots only if its Galois group can be taken apart in steps where each step is abelian. Taking commutator subgroups does exactly that. S₃ and S₄ reach {e}. A₅ is its own commutator subgroup, so the ladder is stuck. A quintic whose Galois group is S₅ therefore has no formula in radicals.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}
Note The group S₅ is the Galois group of x⁵ − 6x + 3 over ℚ. That fact is quoted here, not proved.
