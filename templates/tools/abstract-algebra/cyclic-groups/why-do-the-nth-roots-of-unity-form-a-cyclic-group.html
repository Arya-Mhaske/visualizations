{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Multiply two roots of unity and see where the product lands on the unit circle.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Multiplying complex numbers on the unit circle adds their angles. The nth roots are the points at multiples of 360°/n, so multiplying ω^a by ω^b gives ω^(a+b), with exponents counted mod n. That is exactly addition on a clock with n hours.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}
Note This tool uses the complex numbers from Trigonometry → Polar & Complex.
