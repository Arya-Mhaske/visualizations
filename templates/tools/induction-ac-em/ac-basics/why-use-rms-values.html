Find the steady current that heats the same wire just as fast.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Heat follows i², so a backward current heats as well as a forward one. Over a cycle i² averages to i_m²/2: it is the same curve as its quarter-cycle shift, and the two always add to i_m². The steady current with that heat is i_m/√2. That is the rms value: the root of the mean of the square.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
