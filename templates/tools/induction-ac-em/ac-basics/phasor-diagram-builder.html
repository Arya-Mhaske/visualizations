Step through it, then let it turn.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}In series every part carries the same current, so draw I first. Then each voltage has a fixed place: V_R along I, V_L a quarter turn ahead, V_C a quarter turn behind. Join them tip to tail, and the arrow from start to finish is the source voltage.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Switch off L and C, then R and C, then R and L. Which way does the phase go each time?
  • With all three on, move f until the phase is close to zero. What has happened to V_L and V_C?
  • Find a setting where V_L is larger than the source voltage V. How can that be?
  • Press Play. Does the shape of the diagram change as it turns?
