The Moon is about 60 Earth radii from the centre, so its acceleration is 9.8/60² ≈ 0.0027 m/s², and in 1 s it falls ½ × 0.0027 ≈ 1.4 mm. Yet it keeps moving sideways fast enough that the Earth's surface curves away beneath it by the same amount: it falls round the Earth and never lands.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
