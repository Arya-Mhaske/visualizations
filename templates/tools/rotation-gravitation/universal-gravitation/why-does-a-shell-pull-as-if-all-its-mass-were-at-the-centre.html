{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}A hollow shell of mass M pulls an outside mass as if all of M were at its centre. Inside the shell, the pull is zero.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Add up the pulls of all the rings of the shell. Outside, the nearer rings pull harder and the farther ones weaker, and together they equal M at the centre. Inside, the nearer patches are fewer but closer, the farther patches are more but farther, and these cancel exactly, because of the inverse-square law.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
