{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}The centre of gravity is where the total weight seems to act. It sits at the centre of mass only when gravity has the same value everywhere on the body.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The weight of each piece is m g, and the centre of gravity is the weighted average of positions using these weights. If g is the same for every piece, g cancels and the average is the centre of mass. If g falls with height, the lower pieces weigh a little more and the centre of gravity sits a little below the centre of mass.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}

For ordinary objects the difference is far smaller than the pictures can show, so the effect here is greatly exaggerated.
