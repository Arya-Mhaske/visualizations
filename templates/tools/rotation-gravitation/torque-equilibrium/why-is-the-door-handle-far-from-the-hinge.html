{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Push a door at different distances from the hinge and at different angles. The turning effect, the torque, changes.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Torque τ = r F sin θ. For a given push, the turning effect grows with the distance r from the hinge and is largest when the push is at right angles to the door. A push along the door (θ = 0) or at the hinge (r = 0) does nothing. That is why the handle is placed far from the hinge.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Push at the hinge side (small r). How large is τ?
  • Change θ to 90°. What is the best angle?
  • What push is needed near the hinge to match a push at the handle?
