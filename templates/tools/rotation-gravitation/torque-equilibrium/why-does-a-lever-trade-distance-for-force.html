{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Balance a load on one side of a seesaw with an effort on the other. A long effort arm needs a small force.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Balance means equal turning effects: load × load arm = effort × effort arm. So the effort is smaller than the load by the ratio of the arms. The price is distance: the end where you push moves further than the load rises, by the same ratio.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Make the effort arm 4 times the load arm. What is the effort?
  • How far must you push the end to lift the load 1 cm?
  • Put the pivot at the centre. What is the effort?
