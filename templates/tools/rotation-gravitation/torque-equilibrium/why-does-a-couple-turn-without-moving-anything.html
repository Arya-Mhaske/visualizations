{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Two equal and opposite forces act along different lines. The body turns but its centre stays put.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The forces cancel, so the net force is zero and the centre of mass does not accelerate. But they act on different lines, so their torques add: F × d, where d is the distance between the lines. This torque is the same about any pivot you choose, so a couple's torque is a single fixed number.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Double the gap between the forces.
  • Make the forces act along one line. What is the torque?
