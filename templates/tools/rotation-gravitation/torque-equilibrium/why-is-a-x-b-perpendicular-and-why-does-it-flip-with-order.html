{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Two vectors a and b span a parallelogram. Their cross product is a third vector at right angles to the plane, and it changes sign if you swap the order.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The size of a × b is ab sin θ, the area of the parallelogram. Its direction is set by the right-hand rule: curl the fingers from the first vector to the second and the thumb gives the direction. Swap the order and the curl runs the other way, so b × a = −(a × b).{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
