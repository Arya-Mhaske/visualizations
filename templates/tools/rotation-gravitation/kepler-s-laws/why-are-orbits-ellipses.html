The Sun sits at one of the pins.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The pencil keeps the sum of its two distances to the pins equal to the string length, 2a. That is the definition of an ellipse, and the pins are its foci. Moving the pins together makes a circle (e = 0); moving them apart makes the ellipse longer and thinner.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
