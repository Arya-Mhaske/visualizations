{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}Hang a spinning bicycle wheel from one end of its axle. It does not fall; it swings slowly round in a horizontal circle.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}The wheel's weight gives a torque that is horizontal and at right angles to the spin angular momentum L. Since dL/dt = τ, the torque does not make L bigger; it turns it sideways. So the axle turns round slowly at Ω = mgr/(Iω), the precession rate. A faster spin gives slower precession.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
