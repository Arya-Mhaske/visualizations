{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}A particle moves at constant velocity along a straight line. Its angular momentum about a fixed point stays the same, even though its position changes.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}L = r × mv = m v r sin θ. The quantity r sin θ is the perpendicular distance d from the point to the line of motion, which never changes. So L = m v d is constant for a particle with no force on it. Angular momentum depends on the line, not on where the particle is on the line.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Slide the particle along its path. Does L change?
  • Increase d. What happens to L?
  • Set d = 0. What is L?
