{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}sinh and cosh behave like sin and cos, with one sign flipped.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Since sinh x = (eˣ − e⁻ˣ)/2, the derivative of e⁻ˣ is −e⁻ˣ, and the two minus signs cancel: (sinh x)′ = (eˣ + e⁻ˣ)/2 = cosh x. For cosh x the sign stays minus, so (cosh x)′ = sinh x. In trigonometry (cos x)′ = −sin x; here there is no minus.{% endblock %} {% block notes %}
Note sinh and cosh are defined from eˣ, not from a circle. They sit on a hyperbola, which is where their names come from.
