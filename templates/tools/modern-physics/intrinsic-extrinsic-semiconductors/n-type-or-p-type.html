Silicon makes four bonds, so count the dopant's valence electrons against those four and decide what is left over or missing.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Silicon needs four electrons for its four bonds. A pentavalent atom (P, As, Sb) brings five, so one is spare and free: electrons are the majority carrier, and the crystal is n-type. A trivalent atom (B, Al, In) brings three, so one bond lacks an electron: holes are the majority carrier, and the crystal is p-type.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
