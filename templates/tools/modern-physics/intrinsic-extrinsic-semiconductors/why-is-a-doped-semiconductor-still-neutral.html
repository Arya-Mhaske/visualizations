{% extends "tools/base_tool.html" %} {% block lead %}A donor gives away an electron, so you might expect an n-type crystal to be charged. Count the charges in a patch of crystal.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}A donor atom starts neutral. When it gives up its extra electron, it becomes a fixed + core and the electron roams free. Charge only moved from one place to another, so the cores and the free electrons cancel exactly. A crystal only becomes charged if you take carriers out.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
  • Add dopant atoms one at a time. After each, what is the net charge?
  • Pull out two carriers. What charge is left behind, and where does it sit?
  • Switch to acceptors. Which sign is the fixed core now, and which sign are the free carriers?
