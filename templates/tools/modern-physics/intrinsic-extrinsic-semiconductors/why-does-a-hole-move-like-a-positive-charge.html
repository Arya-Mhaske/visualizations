A neighbour steps in to fill the gap, then the next one, and the next. Count which way the gap travels.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Every electron hops against the field, one place at a time. Each hop leaves the gap one place further along the field. Adding up the charge times the distance of all those hops gives exactly one + charge moving along the field. So the hole acts like a positive charge, even though only electrons ever move.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Turn the field round. Which way does the hole go now? Which way do the electrons go?
  • Look at the formula. Why does a hole moving 5 places equal 5 electrons moving 1 place each?
