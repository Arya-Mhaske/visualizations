Watch what happens to the holes while the crystal settles.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Heat makes electron–hole pairs all the time, and electrons also fall back into holes. At balance the two rates match, and that fixes the product nₑnₕ at nᵢ². Extra electrons from donors raise the recombination rate, so holes disappear faster than heat makes them, until nₕ is about 4.5 × 10⁹ m⁻³ for 1 ppm of arsenic.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

    Note The time unit is arbitrary because recombination is far too fast to watch. The rate model is the simplest one: electrons and holes meet at a rate proportional to n·p, and heat makes pairs at a fixed rate. The balance it gives, n·p = nᵢ², is the law the textbook states.
