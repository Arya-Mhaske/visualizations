Holes and electrons spill across, and each one leaves a fixed ion core behind. Watch what stops the spilling.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Carriers diffuse across because there are many on one side and few on the other. Each one that crosses leaves a charged core behind, so a field grows that pushes carriers back. That backward push is the drift current. The layer grows until drift equals diffusion, and then nothing more crosses. The barrier it leaves is about 0.7 V for silicon.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}

  • Drag the battery to −5 V. How much wider is the layer than at 0 V?
