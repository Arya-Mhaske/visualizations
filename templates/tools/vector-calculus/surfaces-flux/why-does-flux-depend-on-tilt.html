Turn it, and less does.{% endblock %} {% block why_text %}Water crosses the net only if it also crosses the net's shadow as seen from upstream. The shadow has area A cos θ, where θ is the angle between the net's normal n and the stream. So flux is F · n A, the speed times the shadow's area. At 90° the net is edge-on, the shadow is a line, and no water crosses.{% endblock %} {% block try_items %}
